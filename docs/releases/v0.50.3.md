# AelionBot v0.50.3

## 简体中文

### 切换页面和组件时的过渡动画

- 切换 Bot、工作会话或群聊，以及从插件页回到聊天时，消息和顶栏会淡入；打开插件页时整页浮现。
- 设置、模型、设计、用量、插件、定时任务、群聊设置、人格、AI 游戏等处的分页和筛选，切换后内容会轻轻浮现；预览里切换文件时内容淡入。
- 对话框、抽屉、右键菜单、新建菜单、附件菜单、@ 与 / 选择列表、上下文占用面板、提示条和右上角通知，打开和关闭都有动画，关闭时先收起再消失。全屏的工作电脑原地淡入淡出，隐藏电脑卡片时也会淡出。
- 打开设置时，面板的毛玻璃不再先闪一下只模糊遮罩的状态。
- 系统开启了“减少动态效果”时不播放这些动画。

## English

### Transitions when switching pages and components

- Switching Bots, work sessions or group chats, and returning to chat from the plugins page, fades in the messages and the header; the plugins page rises in when it opens.
- Tabs and filters in settings, models, design, usage, plugins, scheduled tasks, group settings, personas and the AI games ease their content in when you switch; switching files in the preview fades the new one in.
- Dialogs, drawers, context menus, the new menu, the attachment menu, the @ and / pickers, the context usage panel, toasts and the notices at the top right animate both when they open and when they close, settling away before they disappear. The full-screen work computer fades in and out in place, and the computer card fades out when hidden.
- Opening settings no longer flashes a frosted panel that blurs only the dimmed backdrop behind it.
- None of this plays when the system's "reduce motion" setting is on.
