# AelionBot v0.39.2

## 简体中文

### 新的输入框

- 输入框收窄到最宽 760px 并居中，悬浮在对话上方，带圆角和柔和阴影；消息列也收窄到同样的宽度，阅读时一行不会拉得太长。
- 去掉了输入框外圈的方形白色底，消息可以从输入框旁边滚过，不会再被一块白色挡住。
- 工作电脑浮窗打开时，消息和输入框在浮窗左侧的区域居中。
- 深色模式下停止按钮的图标更清楚。

### 图片预览

- 打开了多张图片时，可以用图片两侧的箭头或键盘左右方向键切换上一张、下一张，顶部显示当前是第几张。

### 修复

- 打开或关闭停靠预览时，对话不再跳到更早的消息，会停在原来看的位置；原本在最底部的仍停在底部。

## English

### A new composer

- The composer is now at most 760 px wide, centred and floating over the conversation with rounded corners and a soft shadow. The message column narrows to the same width, so lines stay comfortable to read.
- The square white backdrop around the composer is gone; messages scroll past beside it instead of being covered.
- With the work computer card open, messages and the composer centre in the space to its left.
- The stop button's icon is easier to see in dark mode.

### Image preview

- With several images open, switch to the previous or next one with the arrows on either side or the left and right arrow keys; a counter shows which one you are on.

### Fixes

- Opening or closing a docked preview no longer jumps the conversation to earlier messages; it stays where you were reading, and stays at the end if you were there.
