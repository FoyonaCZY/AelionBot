# AelionBot v0.49.0

## 简体中文

### 界面控件

- 按钮、输入框、选择框、开关、标签页、菜单和标签统一成与输入框、顶部悬浮条一致的玻璃风格：主操作是深色胶囊，次要按钮和选中项是浮起的白色玻璃，菜单和下拉列表是磨砂玻璃。
- 去掉输入框选中时的外边框，以及消息气泡右下角的时间。
- 左侧栏的滚动条只在鼠标移到侧栏上时显示。

### 设置

- 修复深色模式下设置页文字后面出现多余底色的问题，各页面恢复为统一的卡片样式。
- 重新设计模型、运行和用量三页：模型分配改为上下排列的卡片；运行设置改为单列，有未保存的更改时底部浮出保存条；用量页的时间范围和筛选放在顶部，概览、趋势、排行和明细都是卡片。

### 处理过程

- Bot 的中间步骤默认折叠成一枚胶囊，运行中和结束后都一样：显示状态和用时、各类操作的图标和次数、增删行数，读过的图片叠放在旁边。运行中只在下方保留 Bot 说的话。
- 点击缩略图直接打开图片，可以用左右箭头或 ← → 切换上一张、下一张。
- 展开后的步骤放在一张玻璃面板上，每一步前面是带颜色的小图标，不再是一列圆圈；思考内容改为柔和的卡片。
- 展开和收起全部步骤、单个步骤和思考内容时有过渡动画。
- 修复展开命令步骤时始终显示“没有文本输出”的问题，现在能看到命令的输出。
- 保存文件也算作改动，计入增删行数，展开后能看到写入的内容。
- 修复展开很长的命令输出会把整个步骤面板撑宽的问题；聊天栏较窄时，胶囊会自动收起次要信息。

## English

### Controls

- Buttons, fields, selects, switches, tabs, menus and tags now share the glass look of the composer and the floating header: primary actions are a dark capsule, secondary buttons and selected items are raised white glass, and menus and dropdowns are frosted glass.
- The composer no longer draws a ring when focused, and message bubbles no longer show the time in their corner.
- The sidebar shows its scrollbar only while the pointer is over it.

### Settings

- Fixed stray backgrounds behind text on the settings pages in the dark theme; every page is back to the same card style.
- Redesigned the Model, Runtime and Usage pages: model assignments are stacked cards; runtime settings are one column with a save bar that floats up while anything is unsaved; on Usage the range and filters sit on top and the overview, trend, rankings and table are cards.

### Run steps

- A Bot's intermediate steps fold into one capsule by default, while it works and after: its status and time, each kind of work as an icon with a count, the lines changed, and the images it read stacked beside it. While it works, only what the Bot says stays out below.
- Clicking a thumbnail opens the image, and the arrows or ← → move to the previous or next one.
- Opened steps sit on one glass panel with a small tinted icon for each step instead of a column of circles; thoughts read as a soft card.
- Opening and closing all steps, a single step and a thought are animated.
- Fixed command steps always saying there was no text output; the command's output now shows.
- Saving a file counts as a change: its lines are added to the totals and the written content shows when the step is opened.
- Fixed a long line of command output widening the whole steps panel; in a narrow chat column the capsule drops its secondary details.
