# AelionBot v0.47.8

## 简体中文

### 连接器

- 企业微信群里的 @ 消息现在会交给 Bot：群消息进入独立的“企业微信 · 群聊”工作会话，最终回答和文件发回群。
- 只允许 owner 和已授权成员触发；陌生人发在群里的消息会被忽略，需要先私聊配对。审批和接管电脑的提示仍发到 owner 的私聊。
- 群消息开头的 @机器人 会被去掉；飞书和微信的群消息仍然不支持。

### 界面

- 修复消息渠道卡片在企业微信 BotID 较长时溢出：开关和“有消息”时间不再压到右侧卡片上，账号会省略号截断。

## English

### Connectors

- WeCom group @mentions now reach the Bot: each group gets its own "WeCom · Group" work session, and the final answer and files go back to the group.
- Only the owner and approved members can wake the Bot; messages from other group members are ignored and they must pair in a private chat first. Approval and takeover prompts still go to the owner's private chat.
- The leading @mention is removed from group text. Feishu and WeChat group messages remain unsupported.

### Interface

- Fixed connector cards overflowing when a WeCom BotID is long: the switch and last-message time no longer spill onto the next card, and the account is truncated with an ellipsis.
