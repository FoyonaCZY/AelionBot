# AelionBot v0.48.7

## 简体中文

### 液态玻璃

- 顶部悬浮条和输入框改为液态玻璃：消息滚过边缘时会像透过厚玻璃一样弯折，中间保持清晰，并带有上沿亮线、发丝边和光泽。
- 设置弹窗和 Bot 资料弹窗使用同样的亮边和光泽。
- 修复安装版里玻璃模糊不生效的问题：顶部悬浮条、输入框、菜单、右侧卡片和预览面板此前只有一层淡色，背后的文字清晰可见。
- 修复设置弹窗背后的界面没有被模糊的问题。

### 对话

- 运行中的中间输出不再显示头像，头像只出现在底部状态和最终回答上。
- 运行中展开“较早的步骤”后，可以点“收起较早的步骤”折叠回去。
- Bot 会在合适的时候使用图表、计算器、指标等消息块展示数据，比如趋势对比，或可以代入自己数字重算的结果；是否使用由 Bot 自行判断。

## English

### Liquid glass

- The chat header and composer are now liquid glass: messages bend as they scroll past the rim, like light through thick glass, while the middle stays clear, with a lit top edge, a hairline rim and a sheen.
- The settings and Bot profile dialogs share the same rim and sheen.
- Fixed glass blur not working in installed builds: the header, composer, menus, side cards and preview panel had only a faint tint, with the text behind them sharp.
- Fixed the app behind the settings dialog not being blurred.

### Conversations

- Interim output during a run no longer shows the avatar; it appears only on the working status and the final answer.
- After opening "earlier steps" during a run, "Hide earlier steps" folds them back.
- Bots now reach for charts, calculators, metrics and other message blocks when they make data clearer, such as trends and comparisons or results you may want to recompute with your own figures; whether to use one is up to the Bot.
