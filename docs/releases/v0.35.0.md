# AelionBot v0.35.0

## 简体中文

v0.34.0 的安装包未能完成发布，本版本包含其全部内容。

### 群聊狼人杀全新界面

- 群聊里的对局卡片直接显示第几天、当前阶段和 12 位玩家的存活情况，轮到你时会标出来。
- 游戏大厅、AI 玩家预设和开局设置重新设计：开局时直接看到这一桌的 12 个座位，点座位就能改这一局的模型和性格，缺人或缺模型时按钮旁会写明原因。
- 对局桌夜晚用深色、白天用浅色，顶部显示阶段进度和倒计时；投票结果整理成票型卡，警长票显示 1.5 倍；选择目标改为头像卡片，发言时可以一键插入座位号或引用自己的查验结果。
- 右侧常驻「我的身份」（按住才显示）和「我的线索」；开局会先揭晓你的身份。
- 对局结束后有战报：公开所有身份和每个人的出局方式，并列出关键事件；运行记录改为从右侧滑出。

### 修复任务运行时的卡顿

- 任务执行期间滚动、点击展开、打字不再延迟几秒才响应。原因是每个工具步骤都会把全部状态同步写盘多次，阻塞了处理输入的主进程；现在连续的改动合并成一次写入，界面状态更新也做了限频。

### 目标和计划卡片

- 执行中的目标默认收起成一行：标题、分段进度条和当前步骤，暂停和更多操作放在右上角。
- 展开后已完成的步骤合成一行，当前步骤高亮并显示验收条件，后面的步骤只列出接下来两步。
- 只有「等待确认」和「需要处理」会自动展开，并直接显示开始或继续按钮；卡住的原因写在卡片里。

### 中间步骤

- 步骤行上直接显示耗时、失败的退出码和搜索命中数。
- 结果改为一条细的信息栏加等宽代码区：JSON 自动格式化并高亮，读取的文件按语言高亮，超过 12 行自动折叠。
- 命令失败时标红并优先显示错误输出。

### 聊天里的图片

- 图片放在文字上方，按原始比例显示；多张图片用两列拼图，超过 4 张显示 +N，悬停可保存。
- 输入框里待发送的图片显示为方形缩略图。

## English

v0.34.0 installers were never published; this release includes all of its changes.

### Redesigned Werewolf in group chats

- The match card in the group chat shows the day, the current phase and which of the 12 players are still in, and marks when it is your turn.
- The lobby, AI player presets and match setup are redesigned: setup shows the table's 12 seats, each seat edits its model and personality in place, and the start button says what is missing.
- The table turns dark at night and light by day, with a phase stepper and countdown; votes are grouped into tally cards with the sheriff's 1.5 weight; targets are avatar tiles, and speeches can insert seat numbers or quote your own checks.
- A side panel keeps your role (shown while held) and your clues; your role is revealed at the start.
- Finished matches get a report with every role, how each player left and the key events; run records open in a side drawer.

### Fix lag while tasks run

- Scrolling, expanding and typing no longer stall for seconds while a task runs. Each tool step wrote the whole state to disk several times, blocking the main process that handles input; changes are now written in one coalesced batch and interface updates are rate-limited.

### Goal and plan cards

- A running goal folds into one line: title, segmented progress and the current step, with pause and more actions at the top right.
- When expanded, finished steps collapse into one line, the current step is highlighted with its acceptance check, and only the next two steps are listed.
- Only cards waiting for confirmation or needing attention open on their own, with the start or continue button and the blocking reason shown in the card.

### Run steps

- Step rows show the duration, a failing exit code and search hit counts.
- Results use a slim info bar and a monospace view: JSON is pretty-printed and highlighted, files are highlighted by language, and output past 12 lines folds.
- Failed commands are marked red and show their error output first.

### Images in chat

- Images appear above the message text at their own aspect ratio; several form a two-column mosaic with +N beyond four, and can be saved on hover.
- Images waiting to be sent show as square thumbnails in the composer.
