# AelionBot v0.51.0

## 简体中文

### 全新的界面风格

- 界面从毛玻璃和渐变背景改成扁平风格：浅灰侧栏接白色聊天区，只有浮在内容上的东西才有阴影。深色模式一起调整。
- 你的气泡和发送按钮用当前 Bot 的颜色，太浅的颜色自动换成深色文字；群聊用默认的蓝色。
- 侧栏顶部是圆形的搜索和新建按钮，底部是你的名字和插件入口，你的头像用默认 Bot 形象。搜索框平时收起，时间在指针移到会话上时显示。
- 聊天顶部是一个 Bot 胶囊，和输入框对齐居中，单击打开 Bot 资料。消息滚动到顶部时渐隐。消息旁不再显示 Bot 头像（群聊保留）。
- 输入框是一整条胶囊，文字和按钮对齐在同一条中线上。
- 电脑、定时任务和设计卡片继续悬浮在聊天区右上角，换成新的白底样式。
- 设置窗口改成整张白色面板。群聊头像去掉了底色。

### 插件改成弹窗

- 插件页改成聊天上方的弹窗：顶部搜索和添加，下面是分类胶囊和两列列表。
- 全部页推荐常用应用，应用页列出全部应用，每个应用可以在自己那一行直接连接。

### 创建 Bot

- 创建和编辑 Bot 的弹窗把头像和名称放在中间，下面可以直接点色点换颜色，取消和保存固定在底部。

### 工作目录只在设置里修改

- 工作目录不再出现在输入框里，“+”菜单也去掉了这一项；把文件夹拖进输入框时只能打包成附件。
- 现在在 Bot 资料、群聊设置或工作会话设置里修改。Bot 资料新增了工作目录一栏，修改后马上保存，从下一个任务开始生效。

### 群聊里的 AI 游戏

- AI 游戏入口从群聊输入框移到右上角的骰子图标。

### 修复

- 设置“运行”页底部的“恢复默认”不再和页面文字重叠：只有存在未保存的修改时，这一栏才浮在内容上方。

## English

### A new look

- The interface moves from frosted glass and gradients to a flat style: a light gray sidebar meets a white conversation, and only what floats over the content has a shadow. Dark mode is updated with it.
- Your bubbles and the send button use the current Bot's color, with dark text when the color is too light for white. Group chats use the default blue.
- The sidebar has round search and new buttons at the top, and your name and the plugins button at the bottom, with the default Bot as your avatar. The search field stays collapsed until you need it, and times show when you point at a conversation.
- The chat header is a Bot chip, centered with the composer, that opens the Bot's profile. Messages fade out as they reach the top. Messages no longer show the Bot's avatar beside them, except in group chats.
- The composer is a single pill, with its text and buttons on one center line.
- The computer, scheduled task and design cards still float at the top right of the chat, in the new white style.
- Settings is now one white sheet. Group avatars no longer have a tile behind them.

### Plugins open in a dialog

- The plugins page is now a dialog over the chat: search and add at the top, then category pills and a two-column list.
- The All view recommends common apps, the Apps view lists them all, and each app connects from its own row.

### Creating a Bot

- The Bot dialog puts the avatar and name in the middle, with color swatches right below them, and keeps cancel and save at the bottom.

### The workspace changes only in settings

- The workspace no longer appears in the composer or its "+" menu, and a folder dropped on the composer can only be packed as an attachment.
- Change it in the Bot profile, group settings or work session settings. The Bot profile gains a workspace row, which saves at once and applies from the next task.

### AI games in group chats

- The AI games entry moves from the group composer to a dice icon at the top right.

### Fixes

- "Restore defaults" at the bottom of the Runtime settings page no longer overlaps the page text: the bar floats over content only while there are unsaved changes.
