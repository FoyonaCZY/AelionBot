# AelionBot v0.55.2

## 简体中文

### 本机电脑控制支持 macOS，并能在后台进行

- “让 Bot 操作这台电脑上的应用”现在也支持 macOS。它需要“辅助功能”和“屏幕录制”两项系统权限：“设置 → 权限”的电脑控制卡片会显示这两项是否已允许，点“去授权”跳到系统设置里对应的页面。授予屏幕录制后需要重新打开 AelionBot。
- 查看窗口、按控件点击按钮和勾选框、填写输入框、滚动，这些不再占用前台。窗口留在原处，你可以继续用电脑，屏幕上也没有提示。macOS 上启动应用、从菜单栏选择命令同样在后台进行。
- 只有按坐标点击、拖动、输入文字和按快捷键时才用真实的鼠标和键盘。这时目标窗口来到前台，屏幕边缘出现边框、顶部出现提示条，按 `Esc` 立即停止。提示出现后 Bot 会停顿片刻再动手，让你来得及松开键盘。
- 浏览器和基于浏览器内核的应用被完全挡住或最小化后不再刷新画面，Bot 要看它时会先把它带到前台。macOS 上所有被完全挡住或最小化的窗口都这样处理。
- macOS 上终端、脚本编辑器和自动操作不能被操作；`Cmd+Tab`、`Cmd+空格`、强制退出、锁屏和切换桌面这类会离开当前应用的快捷键不会发送。

### 生图

- 让 Bot 一次生成超过 10 张图，不会再在第 11 张出错。一条回复最多带 10 个附件，放不下的图照样生成并保存，Bot 也知道它们没有附在回复里。之前第 11 张图其实已经生成并计费，然后才报错。语音合成和附加文件也做了同样的处理。
- 生图的参考图除了聊天里收到的图片，还可以是 Bot 工作目录里的图片，或你电脑上的图片（读取本机文件沿用现有权限），最多 4 张。网上的图可以让 Bot 先下载下来，再用作参考图。

### 设置更简洁

- 设置里各项下面的说明小字去掉了，一行就是标题和控件。实时状态、大小和数量、错误提示、特殊取值的含义，以及不可撤销操作前的警告仍然保留。

## English

### Computer Use on this computer: macOS, and work in the background

- "Let Bots use apps on this computer" now works on macOS too. It needs two system permissions, Accessibility and Screen Recording: the Computer Use card in Settings → Permissions shows whether each is granted, and "Allow…" opens the matching page of System Settings. Reopen AelionBot after granting Screen Recording.
- Looking at a window, clicking buttons and checkboxes by their control, filling in fields and scrolling no longer take the foreground. The window stays where it is, you can keep using the computer, and nothing shows on screen. On macOS, launching apps and choosing commands from the menu bar happen in the background too.
- Only clicking at coordinates, dragging, typing text and pressing shortcuts use the real mouse and keyboard. The target window then comes to the front, a border and a banner appear, and `Esc` stops at once. The Bot pauses briefly after the banner appears, so you have time to let go of the keyboard.
- Browsers and browser-based apps stop redrawing when fully covered or minimized, so the Bot brings one to the front before looking at it. On macOS every fully covered or minimized window is handled this way.
- On macOS, Terminal, Script Editor and Automator can't be operated, and shortcuts that leave the current app (`Cmd+Tab`, `Cmd+Space`, Force Quit, locking the screen, switching desktops) are not sent.

### Image generation

- Asking a Bot for more than 10 images no longer fails at the 11th. A reply carries at most 10 attachments; images past that are still made and kept, and the Bot knows they are not in the reply. Before, the 11th image was generated and billed, and only then reported as failed. Speech and attached files are handled the same way.
- Reference images can now be pictures in the Bot's work directory or on your computer (read with your existing permissions), not only images received in the chat; four at most. For a picture on the web, the Bot can download it first and use it as a reference.

### Simpler settings

- The small explanatory text under settings is gone: a row is its title and its control. Live status, sizes and counts, errors, the meaning of special values, and the warning before an action that can't be undone remain.
