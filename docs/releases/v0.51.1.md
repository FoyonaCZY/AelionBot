# AelionBot v0.51.1

## 简体中文

### 聊天顶部

- 顶部的 Bot 胶囊加大了内边距，头像和文字不再贴着边缘。
- 胶囊和右上角的按钮整体下移，离窗口顶部更远；胶囊也稍微往左挪，避开右侧按钮造成的偏重感。

### 消息区

- 消息滚动经过输入框下方时不再发白，会一直延伸到窗口底边。

### Mac 只支持 Apple 芯片

- 从 v0.51.0 起，Mac 版只提供 Apple 芯片（arm64）安装包。
- Intel Mac 无法再更新，会停留在 v0.50.3。
- 如果你在 Apple 芯片的 Mac 上装的是 Intel 版，自动更新不会替你换包，需要手动下载 arm64 安装包替换。

## English

### Chat header

- The Bot chip at the top has more padding, so the avatar and text no longer sit against its edge.
- The chip and the buttons at the top right sit lower, further from the top of the window. The chip also moves slightly left to balance the buttons on the right.

### Messages

- Messages no longer turn white as they scroll past below the composer. They now run on to the bottom edge of the window.

### Macs need Apple silicon

- Since v0.51.0, the Mac build ships for Apple silicon (arm64) only.
- Intel Macs can no longer update and stay on v0.50.3.
- If you installed the Intel build on an Apple silicon Mac, auto-update will not switch it for you. Download the arm64 installer and replace the app manually.
