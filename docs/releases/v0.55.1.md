# AelionBot v0.55.1

## 简体中文

### 对话导航条

- Bot 对话的左侧多了一列短线，一轮对话一条。说得越多线越长，正在看的那一轮是深色的长线。
- 鼠标停在一条线上，会显示这一轮你说了什么、Bot 回了什么。点线就跳到那一轮，较早、还没加载出来的对话也能跳到。
- 卡片右上角的书签可以标记这一轮，标记过的线会用 Bot 的颜色显示。标记只有你看得到，不会告诉 Bot，重启后仍然保留。
- 也可以用键盘：Tab 进入导航条，上下方向键切换，回车跳转。
- “设置 → 外观 → 对话 → 对话导航条”里可以选“随系统音频律动”，让导航条跟着电脑正在播放的声音起伏，也可以选静态显示或不显示。默认是静态显示。
- 打开律动后，macOS 第一次会请求“系统录音”权限。应用只读取音量，不录制、不保存。窗口最小化或隐藏时会停止读取。Linux 暂不支持。

## English

### Conversation rail

- A Bot chat has a column of short lines along its left edge, one per turn. Turns that said more have longer lines, and the turn you are reading is a longer dark one.
- Hover a line to see what you said in that turn and what the Bot answered. Click it to jump there, including earlier turns that have not loaded yet.
- The bookmark at the top right of the card marks the turn, and marked lines take the Bot's color. Marks are only for you: the Bot is not told, and they stay after a restart.
- It works from the keyboard too: Tab to the rail, the up and down arrows to move, Enter to jump.
- In Settings → Appearance → Conversation → Conversation rail, choose "Move with system audio" to have the rail move with what the computer is playing, or keep it static or hide it. Static is the default.
- With the motion on, macOS asks once for System Audio Recording permission. The app reads sound levels only; nothing is recorded or kept. Reading stops while the window is minimized or hidden. Linux isn't supported yet.
