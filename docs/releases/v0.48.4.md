# AelionBot v0.48.4

## 简体中文

### 界面材质

- 侧栏、对话卡片、插件页和右侧电脑、定时任务卡片改为实色无边框，靠双层阴影悬浮。
- 玻璃效果只用于浮在内容上的层：顶部标题条、输入框、新建菜单、附件菜单、设置弹窗和 Bot 资料弹窗。模糊和饱和度更高，叠加细微噪点，不再有白色描边和内高光。
- 搜索框、工作区、权限和模型按钮改为无边框的浅色填充；设置里的输入框和下拉框也去掉边框，聚焦时显示光圈。
- 窗口背景加入低饱和的色彩光斑，顶部保持纯色，Windows/Linux 窗口按钮区与页面颜色一致。
- 深色模式下弹窗遮罩改为压暗背景；系统开启“降低透明度”时，玻璃层改为实色。

### 对话

- 运行过程的三步预览把思考内容也计入步数。

## English

### Interface materials

- The sidebar, conversation card, plugins page and the right-hand computer and schedule cards are now solid and borderless, lifted by a two-layer shadow.
- Glass is reserved for layers that float over content: the chat header, composer, new-conversation and attachment menus, the settings dialog and the Bot profile dialog. They use stronger blur and saturation with a faint grain, without white outlines or inner highlights.
- Search fields and the workspace, permission and model chips use borderless tinted fills; settings inputs and selects are borderless too and show a focus ring.
- The window background has soft colour fields while the top stays solid, so the Windows/Linux caption area matches the page.
- In dark mode the dialog backdrop dims the screen; with Reduce Transparency enabled, glass layers become solid.

### Conversations

- The three-step run preview now counts thinking toward the step limit.
