# AelionBot v0.50.0

## 简体中文

### 斗地主与麻将

- AI 游戏新增斗地主（三人叫分，炸弹、王炸、春天翻倍）和麻将（推倒和，136 张无花，可碰、杠、和，按番计分）。真人可以和群里的 Bot 同桌，也可以只看 AI 对局。
- 牌桌上的扑克牌和麻将牌都重新绘制：扑克牌有角标、大号花色和人头牌面板，麻将牌是象牙色牌面配绿色牌背，筒、条按传统排布和配色画出。
- 斗地主有记牌器和出牌提示；麻将点一张牌就能看到打出后的进张或听牌，再点一次打出。
- AI 默认只出牌、不说话，避免无意中透露牌情；开局前可以打开“AI 出牌时附带闲聊”。
- AI 也有思考时限，是真人的两倍：斗地主叫分 60 秒、出牌 90 秒，麻将出牌 60 秒、碰杠和 30 秒。到时间没出牌，或者多次给出无效的牌，就按超时规则自动出牌，对局不会暂停。

### 游戏界面

- 重新设计游戏大厅、开局设置、牌桌和运行记录，按钮和面板更简洁；群聊里的对局结果卡片也一并更新。

### 图片请求

- 发给模型的截图改为同一画面的较小 JPEG；请求里的图片超过 20 MiB 时省略较早的图片。服务返回“请求过大”（HTTP 413）时，先去掉旧图片重试，仍失败时给出明确提示，工作记录和原始图片都会保留。

## English

### Dou Di Zhu and Mahjong

- AI games add Dou Di Zhu (three players with bidding; bombs, rockets and a spring double the score) and Mahjong (136 tiles without flowers; pung, kong and win, scored in fan). You can sit at the table with the group's Bots or watch an all-AI match.
- The playing cards and tiles are drawn anew: cards have corner indexes, a large suit mark and framed court cards; tiles have an ivory face on a green back, with circles and bamboo in their traditional layouts and colours.
- Dou Di Zhu has a card counter and play hints. In Mahjong, select a tile to see what drawing chances or waits it leaves, and select it again to discard it.
- By default the AI players only play and stay quiet, so a stray remark cannot give a hand away; turn on "AI 出牌时附带闲聊" before the match to let them talk.
- AI players are on the clock too, with twice a person's time: 60 seconds to bid and 90 to play in Dou Di Zhu, 60 to discard and 30 to claim in Mahjong. Past that, or after repeated unusable moves, they make the timeout move and the match goes on instead of pausing.

### Games interface

- Redesigned the games lobby, match setup, tables and run record with simpler buttons and panels, along with the match result cards in the group chat.

### Image requests

- Screenshots go to the model as a smaller JPEG of the same frame, and older images are left out once a request carries more than 20 MiB of them. When a service rejects a request as too large (HTTP 413), it is retried without the older images and then fails with a clear message; the work record and the original images are kept.
