# AelionBot v0.39.4

v0.39.3 漏掉了形象系统的代码，这一版把它补上，并修好了预览顶栏。

## 简体中文

### Bot 形象

- 新增 36 个 Q 版形象，分为动漫、影视、职业、奇幻与萌物四类。每个形象都沿用 Bot 原有的圆脸和斜眼，加上标志性的发型、帽子和配饰，均为原创致敬设计。
- 形象会随 Bot 的状态动起来：思考时动作慢，工作时动作快，等待你回应时会轻轻跳一下；双马尾、猫耳、蝴蝶结、烟斗烟雾等配饰各有动作。静止时不动，开启「减少动态效果」后动画全部关闭。
- 头像编辑器以形象为主：打开后直接按分类选择形象，纯色、渐变、拼色收在下方的「自定义配色」里。
- 新建 Bot 时随机的头像也会从这些形象中选。
- 旧版本读到形象数据时，会按 Bot 的主色显示为普通头像，不会出错。

### 修复

- 预览和设计画布的顶栏铺满整行：打开本地页面时，模式切换和操作按钮回到最右侧，不再在右边留出一大块空白。
- 画布缩放比例完整显示「100% · 默认」，不再被压成一两个字符。

## English

v0.39.3 left out the character avatar code; this release adds it and fixes the preview header.

### Bot characters

- 36 chibi characters in four groups: anime, film & TV, jobs, and fantasy & cuties. Each keeps the Bot's round face and slanted eyes and adds a signature hairstyle, hat or accessory; all are original tributes.
- Characters move with the Bot's state: slowly while thinking, quickly while working, with a small hop while waiting for you. Twin tails, cat ears, bows, pipe smoke and other accessories have their own motion. They stay still when idle, and all motion stops when Reduce motion is on.
- The avatar editor now leads with characters, grouped by category; solid, gradient and split colours sit under Custom colours below.
- The random avatar for a new Bot can now be one of these characters.
- Older versions show a character as a plain avatar in the Bot's main colour.

### Fixes

- The preview and design canvas header spans the full width: with a local page open, the mode switch and actions sit at the right edge instead of leaving a blank strip.
- The canvas zoom shows "100% · Default" in full instead of a clipped stub.
