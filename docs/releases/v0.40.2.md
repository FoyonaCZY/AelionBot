# AelionBot v0.40.2

这一版修复部分用户双击后长时间不出现窗口的问题。

## 简体中文

### 启动

- 修复双击启动后一两分钟都看不到窗口、后台却一直在运行的问题。启动时应用会把聊天记录同步进本地搜索索引；以前只要前面有一条消息被移动或删除（例如私聊消息归位），后面的全部记录都会被重新索引，消息多时要一分多钟。现在只更新变化的记录，同样的情况不到 1 秒。
- 运行中整理记录时也不会再因为同样的原因卡住。
- 升级后第一次启动会自动转换一次索引记录，不需要手动处理，聊天记录和搜索结果不变。

## English

### Startup

- Fixed the window not appearing for a minute or two after launch while the app kept running in the background. At startup the app syncs chat history into its local search index; moving or removing one early message (filing a private chat, for example) used to re-index every later record, over a minute with a long history. Only changed records are updated now, in under a second.
- The same cause no longer freezes the app while it runs.
- The first start after updating converts the index records once, automatically. Chat history and search results are unchanged.
