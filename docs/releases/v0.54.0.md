# AelionBot v0.54.0

## 简体中文

### 在手机上使用 AelionBot

- 新增手机网页 <https://app.aelion.chat>，可以添加到主屏幕，用起来和 App 一样。Bot 仍然在电脑上运行，手机用来查看和操作。
- 开启方法：在电脑上打开“设置 → 手机访问”，打开开关后点“添加手机”，用手机扫码，再在电脑上点“允许”。iPhone 要先在 Safari 里“添加到主屏幕”，再从主屏幕打开扫码，这样才能收到通知（iOS 16.4 以上）。
- 手机上可以：
  - 在首页看每个 Bot 在做什么；需要你批准的操作放在最上面，可以直接允许
  - 在对话、工作会话和群聊里发消息、照片和文件，和电脑同步
  - 用语音输入，或朗读回复
  - 切换和新建会话，开关自动化、立即运行
  - 查看工作电脑的画面；遇到登录、验证码时接管，处理完交还给 Bot
- 需要你批准或回答、任务完成或失败、自动化有结果时，手机会收到通知。你在电脑前操作时，任务完成不会推送到手机。
- 电脑离线时写的消息先存在手机上，电脑上线后自动发送。
- 手机和电脑在同一个网络时会自动直连，画面和文件不再经过中继。

### 安全

- 手机和电脑之间端到端加密，中继只转发密文，也看不到通知内容。
- 每部手机都要在电脑上点“允许”才能连接，在“手机访问”里可以随时移除。
- 手机上可以打开“打开时验证身份”，每次连接都要先通过 Face ID、指纹或手机密码。
- 手机上不能修改模型、插件、API Key 和连接器，不能打开终端，也不能把本机权限切到“完全放行”。

### 其他

- 开启手机访问后，关闭窗口时应用会留在托盘里；默认不让电脑睡眠，可以在“手机访问”里关掉。
- 中继可以自己部署，方法见仓库里的 `relay/README.md`。部署后在“手机访问 → 中继”里填入地址，已配对的手机需要重新配对。

## English

### AelionBot on your phone

- A new phone web app at <https://app.aelion.chat> can be added to the home screen and works like an app. Bots keep running on your computer; the phone is for watching and steering them.
- To set it up, open Settings → Phone access on the computer, turn it on and click Add a phone. Scan the code with your phone, then click Allow on the computer. On iPhone, first choose Add to Home Screen in Safari and scan from the home screen app; notifications only work there (iOS 16.4 or later).
- On the phone you can:
  - see what each Bot is doing on the home screen, with actions waiting for your approval at the top, ready to allow
  - send messages, photos and files in chats, work sessions and group chats, kept in sync with the computer
  - talk instead of typing, and have replies read aloud
  - switch sessions or start new ones, and turn automations on or off or run them now
  - watch the work computer's screen, take over for sign-ins and captchas, and hand it back to the Bot
- Your phone is notified when a Bot needs approval or an answer, when a task finishes or fails, and when an automation has results. Finished tasks aren't pushed to the phone while you're using the computer.
- Messages written while the computer is offline wait on the phone and are sent once it's back.
- When the phone and computer share a network they connect directly, so the screen and files skip the relay.

### Security

- Everything between phone and computer is end-to-end encrypted. The relay only forwards ciphertext and can't read notifications either.
- Each phone must be allowed on the computer before it can connect, and can be removed under Phone access at any time.
- Turn on "Verify when opening" on the phone to require Face ID, a fingerprint or the phone's passcode on every connection.
- The phone can't change models, plugins, API keys or connectors, can't open a terminal, and can't switch local permissions to full access.

### Other changes

- While phone access is on, closing the window keeps the app in the tray. By default the computer is kept awake; you can turn this off under Phone access.
- You can host the relay yourself; see `relay/README.md` in the repository. Enter its address under Phone access → Relay, then pair your phones again.
