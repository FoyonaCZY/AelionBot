# AelionBot v0.58.1

## 简体中文

### 视频工作室

- Bot 剪辑已有视频时优先用视频工作室：`video_project_create` 可以直接传入附件或文件路径，按原视频的尺寸和帧率建好项目，画面和声音分别放在主视频轨和配音轨。裁剪、拼接、加字幕、混音、变速和转格式都在工作室里完成，只有工作室做不到的事才用 ffmpeg 等命令行工具。
- 拖动时间轴上的片段时，其它片段会实时让开，并用虚框标出落点：主视频轨插入后自动补齐，其它轨道推过相邻片段的中点就交换位置。声音跟着画面一起移动，画面预览与时间轴一致，整个调整可以一步撤销。
- 修复播放时点击时间轴会提示 “Render IR frame queue reached its 2 request limit” 并卡住的问题。
- 工作室的模式切换、撤销、重做和导出并入预览面板的标题栏，不再占两行；视频项目的“批注”上显示评论数量。
- “一键去静音”会同时剪画面和对应的声音，完成后提示去掉了多少秒，找不到静音或没选片段时也会说明原因。
- “自动压低背景音”和“响度标准化”移到导出面板的“声音”一栏，改成可以勾选和取消的选项，并注明只在导出时处理。

### 对话

- 消息很多时，左侧书签条不再拉得很长：超出部分在条内滚动，两端淡出，当前阅读的位置保持在中间。
- 打开文件预览时隐藏书签条。

## English

### Video Studio

- Bots edit existing videos in Video Studio first: `video_project_create` takes an attachment or a file path and sets the project up at the video's own size and frame rate, with the picture on the main track and the sound on the voice track. Trims, joins, captions, mixing, speed changes, and format conversions happen in the studio; command-line tools such as ffmpeg are kept for what the studio cannot do.
- Dragging a clip on the timeline moves the other clips out of its way as you go, with an outline where it will land: the main track opens a gap and closes up behind it, and other tracks swap two clips once one is pushed past the middle of its neighbour. Sound moves with its picture, the picture preview matches the timeline, and the whole rearrangement is one undo step.
- Fixed clicking the timeline during playback showing "Render IR frame queue reached its 2 request limit" and freezing playback.
- The studio's mode switch, undo, redo, and export now sit in the preview panel's header instead of a second bar; the comment count shows on a video project's Annotate tab.
- Remove silence cuts the picture and its sound together and reports how much it removed, or why it could not.
- Auto-ducking and loudness normalization moved to a Sound section of the export popover as options you can turn on and off, noting that they apply on export.

### Chat

- With many messages, the bookmark rail on the left stays short: the rest scrolls inside it, fading at the ends, with the message being read kept in the middle.
- The rail is hidden while a file preview is open.
